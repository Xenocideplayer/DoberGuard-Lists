# DoberGuard and DoberGuard Shield privacy policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

DoberGuard is built so that your calls, texts and contacts never leave your phone. This
policy explains exactly what each app does with your information.

## DoberGuard (the spam call and text blocker and texting app)

**DoberGuard has no internet access.** The app does not request Android's internet
permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There are no accounts, no analytics, no
ads, no crash reporting services and no servers.

What it uses on your phone, and why:

- **Text messages (SMS and MMS):** DoberGuard is your texting app, so it reads, sends and
  stores your texts on your phone like any texting app, and checks incoming texts for scams.
- **Contacts:** read on the phone so texts and calls from people you know are never filtered,
  and to show names.
- **Call history (optional):** read on the phone so numbers you've called or talked to aren't
  treated as strangers, and to show caller hints like "called you 3 times this week".
- **Call screening:** Android lets DoberGuard decide whether an incoming call from a number
  that isn't in your contacts rings, goes to voicemail, or is blocked. It sees the number and
  whether the carrier verified it, nothing else.
- **Microphone (optional):** only while you record a voice message.
- **Your phone number:** used on the phone to leave you out of your own group chats.
- **Fingerprint or screen lock (optional):** checked by Android for app lock; DoberGuard never
  sees your fingerprint.
- **NFC (optional):** only while you're on a "tap phones together" screen.

Texts DoberGuard sends for you:

- **"Report to your carrier":** if you choose it, the spam text and number are forwarded to
  7726, the free carrier spam-reporting number.
- **"Ask unknown callers to text" (optional, off by default):** sends a short text you can
  edit to a declined caller, at most once a day per number.
- **Family mode (optional):** texts a weekly summary of counts (for example "blocked 9 spam
  calls") and occasional alerts to one helper you choose. These never include what any message
  said, or who called or texted you. Every text sent to the helper also appears on your phone.

What stays on your phone: your settings, block and allow lists, caught texts, the archive,
what the filter learns from your taps, and the weekly counts. Backups are files you create and
put where you choose; they can be locked with a password.

## DoberGuard Shield (the companion scam-site and ad blocker)

Shield is the only DoberGuard app that goes online, and only for two things:

- **Downloading block lists:** a public list of scam, phishing and ad sites, downloaded from
  GitHub about once a day. The download request includes nothing about you.
- **Looking up websites:** Shield uses Android's local VPN feature to check the site names your
  apps look up against those lists, on the phone. Allowed lookups are passed to the DNS provider
  you pick (Quad9 by default, or Cloudflare). Shield doesn't see the content of what you browse.

The tracking report (which apps looked up trackers) is counted on your phone and never sent
anywhere. Shield Plus keys are checked on the phone, without contacting any server.

## Children

DoberGuard is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from
anyone.
